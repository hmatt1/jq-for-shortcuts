Privacy Policy

JQ for Shortcuts collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it contains no networking code, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

Filters run on your iPhone or iPad. The JSON you give the app, in the Playground, the share sheet or a Shortcuts action, is read on the device and not kept after the run.

The app stores your saved filters, their sample inputs and your settings in its own container on the device. Like any app's data, they are part of your device's own backup.

Input history is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the app keeps the filters, arguments and inputs of the last 10 runs from Shortcuts, so you can load them in the Playground. An input over 1 MB is not kept, and the history never grows past 20 MB. History is left out of device backups. Clear Input History in Settings deletes it at once, and turning history off deletes it too.

Other apps

Shortcuts decides what JSON it passes to the app and what it does with the results. Fetching data, for example with Get Contents of URL, happens in Shortcuts, not in this app.
